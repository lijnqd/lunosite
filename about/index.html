#about us

lunö is a design studio that gives visuals a meaning. we make brand identities, websites, and digital products for startups and growing businesses

most of our clients come to us with the same problem: they’re great at what they do, but their brand or website doesn’t show it. people don’t get what they offer, or don’t trust it enough to reach out. that’s the part we fix

we don’t start with pretty pictures. we start by asking questions: who your customers are, what they need, what makes you different. then we turn all of that into a brand and a website that feel clear and honest, and that look like you
