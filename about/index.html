#about us

lunö is an independent design studio. we make brand identities, logos and websites for startups and growing businesses across europe and the us. over the last 4 years we’ve designed everything from one-page landings to large digital systems.
we work with any business that makes something real, from toothbrushes to travel agencies. just not casinos or get-rich-quick courses.

#how to work with us

what working with us feels like

you talk to a real person

no managers in the middle and no long email chains. you work directly with the designer

meaning in every detail

we start with the idea and carry it through ascii, glyphs, interface and code, so nothing gets lost along the way

no surprises

you’ll know the price, the timeline, and what you’re getting before we start. if something can’t be done, we’ll tell you

you don’t need a perfect brief

just tell us what you’re working on, and we’ll figure out the rest together
