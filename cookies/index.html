cookie policy

#what are cookies

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage.

#how we use them

Necessary. Keep the site working and remember your cookie choice. These are always on.

Preferences. Remember your settings.

Analytics. Help us understand how the site is used (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics). They run only if you accept them.

#what is stored

  • luno_consent – your cookie choice (local storage, until you change it)
  • luno_pre – remembers that you have already seen the intro animation (session storage, until you close the tab)
  • _ga, _ga_* – Google Analytics, to tell visitors apart and count visits (up to 2 years, analytics only)

#your choice

Until you accept, analytics stay off. You can change your mind at any time:

open cookie settings

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may then work differently.
