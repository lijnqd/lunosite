#faq

here are a few things you might be wondering about

discuss a project

brand identity, websites and landing pages, website & ux/ui design and development — separately or as one end-to-end project

from founders launching their first landing page to growing companies that need a new brand and a website that works

that’s completely fine — most of our clients don’t, at first. bring a feeling, a few screenshots or just your story. we’ll ask the right questions, gather references and build a visual concept together. you’ll see the direction before we design anything.

you won’t be surprised at the end — you approve the concept first, and only then we design the rest. every project includes 2–3 rounds of revisions, so we’ll keep refining until it feels right.

we share a clear timeline before we start and keep you updated at every stage. and if a delay on our side pushes the project more than 20% past the agreed date, you get 20% off your next order.

no surprises. we agree on the price and what’s included before we start — in writing. you pay 50% upfront and 50% before the final handover. if you decide to add something along the way, like extra pages or development, we’ll discuss the cost with you first.
