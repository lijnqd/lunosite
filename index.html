#process

how a project goes

brief

a short call or form about your business, audience and goals. you get a fixed quote and a timeline.

concept

we collect references, build a moodboard and show you the visual direction. you approve it before we design any pages.

design & build

we design every page for desktop and mobile, then build it. you see progress every week.

launch

final revisions, all source files handed over, launch and 30 days of free support.

I don’t start with colors. I start with questions: what you do, who buys from you, and why they’d choose you over someone else. Good design makes a brand easy to understand and easy to trust. That’s the job.

— yana s.founder of lunö studio
