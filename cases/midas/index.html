Midas

Multi-page website for a digital marketing agency

task

Midas is a digital marketing agency, and it needed a multi-page website built from scratch.

The brief was demanding: a very bold design without heavy images, animation or video, with many colors that still don’t feel childish, and no slow pages. Each page had to answer one question and let the visitor act without extra clicks, scrolls or waiting. The first screen could take no more than 70–75% of the viewport, so it’s clear there is more below.

what was hard

Lots of text and almost no images: the challenge was to make that much text interesting to read.

The client had almost no ready copy, so we started from placeholder text and wrote most of the pages ourselves.

solution

Instead of photos, 3D objects break up the text, and they stay light, so the pages load fast.

One accent color carries the headings and key elements; secondary colors appear only in some cards, 3D objects and small details. Nothing takes more than one screen, so every page is easy to scan.

result

Every page now answers one question and leads to one action, so visitors get to what they need without extra clicks or waiting. The text reads easily thanks to the 3D accents and a clear color system.

8%conversion rate after the redesignfrom 2%

what we delivered

A multi-page website design, responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile, a UI kit, and the copy for the pages.
