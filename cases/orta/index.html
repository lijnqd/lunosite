Orta Community

#corporate website#ux/ui#web design

Corporate website for a multifunctional space in Astana

#about the project

Orta Community is a multifunctional space in the very heart of Astana, perfectly suited for events, photo and video shoots, and creative projects
Made together with the Uprock agency

#task

Design a modern, stylish website that captures the vibe of the space and its guests: a place where creativity, events and bold ideas come together

#client request

  • As colorful as possible
  • Interesting animations
  • A site that feels like the space itself: bold and creative

#solution

  • Bold, saturated colors and large expressive type, so the site feels as lively as the place
  • Animations that add movement and character without slowing the pages down
  • Blocks for events, photo and video shoots and creative projects, so each type of guest quickly finds what they need

#process

  1. Competitor analysis — 1 day
  2. Searching for references — 4 days
  3. Visual concept — 20 days
  4. Responsive design — 5 days
  5. UI kit — 2 days

#what we delivered

  • Corporate website design
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
  • UI kit
  • Animation concepts

#result

The concept gives Orta Community a digital presence that feels as distinctive as the space itself
A vivid visual system, expressive typography, and carefully paced motion bring its creative atmosphere online

The clear structure helps visitors explore how the space can be used, from hosting events to creating photo and video shoots, while responsive layouts keep the experience cohesive across desktop, tablet, and mobile
