ORTA

Corporate website for a multifunctional space in Astana

task

Orta Community is a multifunctional space in the heart of Astana, made for events, photo and video shoots and creative projects. We designed its website together with the Uprock agency.

The client wanted a site that feels like the space itself: as colorful as possible, with interesting animation, bold and creative, a place where events and ideas come together.

solution

Saturated colors and large expressive type make the site feel as lively as the place, and the animation adds movement and character without slowing the pages down.

Separate blocks for events, shoots and creative projects let every kind of guest find what they need quickly, and the responsive layouts keep it all together on desktop, tablet and mobile.

result

ORTA got a website as lively as the space itself: bright, animated and easy to navigate on any screen. The project received an Uprock award in 2025.

timeline

From the first competitor analysis to the finished UI kit:

32days from the brief to the ui kit
20days on the visual concept
5days on responsive layouts

what we delivered

The corporate website design, responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile, a UI kit and animation concepts. The result is a digital presence as distinctive as the space itself.
