privacy policy

last updated: 13 october 2026

#who we are

This website, designluno.com, is operated by Yana S., an independent designer doing business as lunö studio (“we”, “us”)

We are responsible for deciding how your personal information is used. If you have questions or want to exercise your privacy rights, email us at hey.lunostudio@gmail.com

#what we collect

Contact form. When you send us a message, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, and message. We also receive the date and time of your submission

Technical and security information. When you visit the site, the site and its service providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect it, such as your IP address, browser and device information, and request time. This may include server or security logs and can be processed whether or not you accept analytics cookies

Analytics. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics may collect information about how you use the site, such as pages viewed, interactions, referring website, approximate location, browser and device information, and cookie identifiers. See our cookie policy for details

#how we use your information

We use your information to:

  • reply to your inquiry and discuss your project
  • prepare a proposal or estimate at your request
  • understand how visitors use the site and improve it, if you accept analytics cookies
  • operate, secure, and protect the site and form from spam or abuse

We do not sell your personal information

#legal bases

We use the information you submit through the contact form to respond to your request and, where relevant, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract

We may use technical information to operate and protect the site, prevent spam, and investigate misuse. Where applicable law requires us to identify a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available

We use analytics information only with your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time through cookie settings

#who receives your information

We use service providers to operate the site and handle inquiries:

  • Cloudflare — DNS, security, and handling of the contact form
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Telegram — form submissions are sent to a private chat used by lunö studio so we can receive and respond to inquiries
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics — analytics, enabled only after you accept analytics cookies

These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, the site, or others

#cookies

We use essential storage to operate the site and remember your cookie choice. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them. You can review the cookies and similar technologies we use, and change your choice, in our cookie policy

#how long we keep your information

We keep contact form inquiries for up to 12 months after our last meaningful contact, then delete them or remove details that identify you. If an inquiry becomes a project, we may keep relevant records for as long as needed to manage the project, meet legal obligations, or establish or defend legal claims

We keep Google Analytics data for up to 14 months. The lifetime of an analytics cookie may be different from the retention period for analytics data. You can find cookie durations in our cookie policy

#your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may be able to ask us to:

  • provide access to your personal information
  • correct or delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • provide a copy of information you gave us, where the right to data portability applies
  • stop processing based on consent by withdrawing that consent

To make a request, email hey.lunostudio@gmail.com You may also complain to the data protection authority responsible for your location

#international transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. Where the law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we use an applicable transfer mechanism. You can email us to ask for more information about the transfers relevant to your request

#changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated
